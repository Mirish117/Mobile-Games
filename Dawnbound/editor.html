Brush Overlay
Dawnbound Map Painter

Red = blocked (can't walk). Clear = walkable. Paint with your finger. Everything saves automatically in this browser.

Paint WALK / Paint BLOCK: one finger paints. Use the Brush slider for size. Paint walkable over paths, under arches, behind lamps; paint blocked over walls, water, roofs, cliffs.
Polygon: tap points around an area, then Fill WALK or Fill BLOCK. Good for straight edges like docks and walls.
Pan/Zoom: one finger drags the map. In any mode, two fingers pinch to zoom and drag. (Mouse: wheel zooms, right-drag pans.)
Play-test: opens the game on this map with your edits, so you can walk it straight away. Come back with the browser back button.
Download: saves masks_user.js. To make your edits permanent for everyone: put that file in the game's data folder on GitHub (replace the existing one). Or send it to me and I'll merge it in.

Tip: the thin green line is the edge of what's currently walkable. Keep paths at least a thumb-width (about 4 brush) wide.
