Edits are kept automatically in this browser. To make them permanent for the game, download the files and upload them to the Dawnbound/data folder on GitHub (replacing the old ones).
Dawnbound Map Painter

Collision: paint where characters can walk. Red = blocked. Paint WALK / BLOCK with a finger; Polygon fills shapes. "Reach" tints purple any walkable ground you can't get to from the map's entrances.

Objects: every character, enemy, chest, door and entrance on the map appears as a marker. Tap one to select, drag it to move it. The side panel lets you change what it does. + Add creates new chests, pickups, characters, enemies, signs, doors/entrances and trigger zones, each with actions (say text, give gold or items, heal, warp, run a scene...). Removing a built-in thing just hides it (you can bring it back).

Overlays: for walking under things. Paint over the part of the art (top of an arch, tree canopy, a lamp post, a statue) and it is drawn in front of characters. Always above is for arch tops and canopies. Behind-line is for trunks, statues, posts: a character is hidden only while standing above the depth line. Use the Test tool to drag a little dummy around and see the effect. Move shifts the piece, which makes it a copy of that art somewhere else (new rocks, logs, trees).

View: pinch with two fingers to zoom, drag with two to pan (one finger drags the map in Objects/Overlays when nothing is hit). ▶ Test opens the game at the selected thing or the middle of your view with all your edits applied.
